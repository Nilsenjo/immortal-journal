OUR MISSION

A life doesn't need to be famous
to be extraordinary.

History has preserved the words of emperors, philosophers, and presidents. But what about the billions of ordinary people whose stories have been lost to time?

We believe every life holds experiences, lessons, and wisdom worth preserving.

Our mission is to build an archive of humanity's lives, preserving the stories of individuals for generations to come.

Immortal Journal begins by inspiring people to live fulfilling lives, pursue what matters to them, and document their journeys so their stories can live on long after they're gone.

History remembers the famous. We want to remember everyone.

THE LONGER VIEW · OUR VISION

History remembers the famous.
We want to remember everyone.

Imagine discovering a journal written by your great-great-grandfather 200 years ago.

What was his life like? What did he dream of accomplishing? What challenges did he face? Who did he love? What did an ordinary Tuesday look like in his world?

Not because he was famous or changed the course of history, but simply because he was part of yours.

Now imagine the countless stories like his that have been lost.

Billions of lives have come and gone, taking with them experiences, perspectives, and wisdom that future generations will never know.

We believe those stories deserve to be preserved.

Our ambition is to build the world's largest archive of human life stories — a place where future generations can discover the people who came before them, learn from their experiences, and find inspiration for their own lives.

A private life. A lasting legacy.

We envision a digital journaling platform where people can document their lives privately, in their own words. Their writings would remain private throughout their lifetime and could become part of a public archive after their death, only with their explicit prior authorization and verified passing.

Every life has a story. We want to preserve them all.
