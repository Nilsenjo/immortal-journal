OUR MISSION

A life doesn't need to be famous
to be extraordinary.

History has preserved the words of emperors, philosophers, and presidents. But what about the billions of ordinary people whose stories have been lost to time?

We believe every life holds experiences, lessons, and wisdom worth preserving.

Our mission is to build an archive of humanity's lives, preserving the stories of individuals for generations to come.

Immortal Journal begins by inspiring people to live fulfilling lives, pursue what matters to them, and document their journeys so their stories can live on long after they're gone.

THE LONGER VIEW · OUR VISION

History remembers the famous.
We want to remember everyone.

Imagine being able to read the thoughts of someone who lived 300 years ago. Their ambitions, struggles, adventures, and everyday experiences. Not because they changed the course of history, but simply because they lived it.

Billions of lives have come and gone, taking with them stories, perspectives, and wisdom the world will never know.

We believe those stories deserve to be preserved.

Our ambition is to create the world's largest archive of human life stories — a place where future generations can discover the lives of those who came before them, learn from their experiences, and find inspiration for their own journeys.

A private life. A lasting legacy.

We envision a private digital journaling platform where individuals could document their lives throughout their lifetime. Journals would remain private while their authors are alive. Following verified death, and only with the author’s explicit authorization, their writings could become part of a public archive for future generations.

This is only the beginning.

The physical journal is our only product available today. The digital platform does not exist yet, and purchasing a journal does not enroll you in a digital service. Strong privacy protections, author controls, and death verification must be in place before any future platform can launch. Digital preservation and posthumous publication are not available today.

Every life has a story. We want to preserve them all.
